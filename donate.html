Donate to OpenDoors

OpenDoors is supported by individual, government, and foundation grants. Please consider donating, as every donation helps us continue to fund and expand our services. To speak to a staff member directly about donations, see details below.

Make an online donation through Paypal using the buttons below, or learn about the items we can accept!

Donate to OpenDoors

We are raising funds to purchase necessities for the people we serve such as winter supplies, tents, sleeping bags, food, and emergency rental assistance. 100% of your donations will go directly for these necessities for people that are coming out of prison, living in our shelters, or struggling with homelessness.

Donate to the Stop Torture RI Campaign

Donate to help support our campaign to reform solitary confinement in RI. Funds go to our outreach and organizing work, which is run by survivors of solitary confinement.

Donate Items

We can accept the following items:

  • Winter coats

  • New clothing

  • Hygiene products

  • Blankets

  • Sleeping bags

  • Tents

  • Body warmers

  • Ready-to-eat nonperishable food

To coordinate donations, email dbruce@opendoorsri.org.

Other ways to donate:

Checks or money orders should be made out to "OpenDoors", and can be sent to 485 Plainfield Street, Providence, RI, 02909.

A donation to OpenDoors can make a great gift to mark a special occasion for a friend or loved one. Consider us for holidays, anniversaries, birthdays, or graduations. For information on how to make a gift donation, please contact Patrick Westfall at (401) 214-1818 or via email at pwestfall@opendoorsri.org.

OpenDoors is a 501(c)3 non-profit organization. Donations are tax deductible.
