Digital Literacy

OpenDoors recognizes that learning to use a computer or a smartphone with confidence is an important skill for anyone entering the workforce. We offer a basic introduction to computers class each week.

As part of our Employment Readiness Program, basic computer skills, including using email, searching the Internet, and conducting an organized online job search are offered on a weekly basis.
