Woonsocket Shelter and She Thrives

Jessica Vega

“It was like a miracle. It was like they changed my head.”

In the fall of 2025, Jessica was homeless for the first time in her life, staying at the OpenDoors shelter in Woonsocket and drinking every day. A case manager told her about She Thrives, which pairs housing and recovery support with GLP-1 medication. She didn’t believe it would work. The day of her first injection was the last day she drank.

She has since moved out of the shelter and into an apartment with her daughter, and her children are back in her life.
