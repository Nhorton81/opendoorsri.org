OpenDoors recognizes that finding affordable housing can be one of the biggest obstacles faced by formerly incarcerated individuals. We offer over 300 beds between various housing programs, including group shelters, transitional recovery houses, and permanent supportive housing.

Read our Homeless Management Information System (HMIS) Agency Privacy Policy Notice

 Housing Services

What Types of Housing Are There?

Homeless Shelters

  • Men’s group shelter

  • Pawtucket Shelter

  • Woonsocket Shelter

Transitional Sober Housing

  • 9 Yards House and Phase II House (men’s transitional houses)

  • Foundations (women’s transitional house)

  • Women and Children’s House

Permanent Supportive Housing

  • Section 8 Apartments

How Much Does it Cost?

The group shelter beds are free and the most accessible. For some individuals, our transitional housing beds are also free or subsidized. We also offer self-pay beds for $450-$600 per month.

For more details call 401-781-5808.

How Do I Get On the Shelter Waitlist?

All shelters in the state are filled through this list of seven Regional Access Points.

How Do I Get Into Transitional Recovery Housing?

Grant-funded recovery housing beds are assigned through the 942-STOP system and have a low availability due to demand. Our self-pay recovery housing beds are more likely to be available.

State-wide bed availability is posted on the BHDDH recovery housing waitlist registry. For all other details, call OpenDoors at 401-781-5808.

How Do I Get Into Permanent Housing?

Our Resource Center staff can help you apply for permanent housing options across the state.

OpenDoors’ permanent supportive housing can be applied for in person and has a waitlist of approximately three years.
