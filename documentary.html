GLP-1 Addiction Treatment Documentary

Jessica Vega was living in a shelter after losing her housing due to alcoholism. Then she started taking a GLP-1 treatment for addiction and everything changed. This is an excerpt from a longer 10 minute documentary film about the OpenDoors GLP-1 addiction treatment program that follows Jessica and two other program graduates. More information is available about this program here. Contact OpenDoors at admin@opendoorsri.org if you are interested in hosting a screening of the full documentary. her
