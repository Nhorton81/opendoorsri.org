Employment Services

OpenDoors offers employment case management and readiness classes, both inside and outside the prison. Our employment program is taught by a full-time, experienced job readiness specialist who prepares clients to enter professional employment and become successful workers.

Why Should I Join the Employment Program with Open Doors?

  • Learn computer skills that are essential when filling out online applications and sending your resumes.

  • Our Employment Services Coordinator will sit with you and personally help you craft a professional resume.

  • Your "30 Second Commercial" will help you talk about your criminal record with employers.

  • Practice your interview skills and make yourself stand out and get noticed by employers.

  • Receive guidance on companies that are hiring and local job fairs.

  • Get support and guidance from the experts at helping individuals with records find jobs.

  • You may be eligible for a free monthly RIPTA pass when participating in the program.

  • Receive help reinstating your driver's license and referrals to OpenDoors Trucking, our transitional employment freight company

When Do the Classes Meet?

The classes are scheduled one-on-one from Monday-Friday 8:30 AM- 4:30 PM. We will do our best to accommodate your schedule. There is also a weekly open computer lab Tuesday afternoons available to those enrolled in the program.

How Long Does the Program Last?

The program is here as long as you need it. As long as you are searching for a job you can participate in the program. You may feel like after the first two weeks that you are ready for the job search, or you may feel that you would like some extra support after the first two weeks. It is up to you.

Does the Program Work?

Most people who participate in the program find jobs. In the last year, we've helped hundreds find employment. Watch the video above to learn more.

What If I Already Took the Program While Incarcerated?

That's great! There are some things that we cannot do on the inside. Let OpenDoors help you pick up where the program inside left off. We'll make sure that your resume looks professional and help you with computer skills.

How Do I Sign Up?

To sign up for any programs at OpenDoors, call 401-781-5808 and request an intake appointment. Intake appointments can be conducted over the phone. You can also email info@opendoorsri.org for more information.
