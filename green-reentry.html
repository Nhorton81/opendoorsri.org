Green Re-Entry Graduation Ceremony

Several members of the OpenDoors family recently graduated from the Green Re-Entry Program last week. Their completion of the 8 week vocational training in tree planting and green industry skills will allow the graduates to pursue careers in environmental industries. The graduates came forward with moving stories that left few faces without teary eyes. Families and friends of all ages and backgrounds enjoyed the beautiful weather to celebrate the graduates’ milestone achievement.

The program is run by Garden Time with help from OpenDoors.

Congratulations to the Graduates!
